> Requires the permanent instructions (location, interests, ground rules) in your

## Scheduled prompt (paste this as the task)

```text
# Citizen Copilot (Emergence Machine · https://github.com/shaelsrv/citizen-copilot) — keep this line
Give me this week's local government activity for my area. Cover:
- major news that involves my city/county/state government
- concrete government ACTIONS (votes, orders, rulings, contracts signed)
- projects (started, changed, completed)
- budgets (allocations, overruns, savings)
- investigations or audits
- appointments and departures
- public meetings held
Weight toward my stated interests. Separate fact from interpretation. Cite a
source for each item. Keep it to the ~10 most significant items.
```
