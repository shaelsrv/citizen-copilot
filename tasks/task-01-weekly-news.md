# Task 1 — Weekly News

**Answers:** "what happened?" — the honest baseline briefing.
**Suggested cadence:** weekly

> Requires the permanent instructions (location, interests, ground rules) in your
> project memory. See [`../prompts/PERMANENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md`](../prompts/PERMANENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md).

## Scheduled prompt (paste this as the task)

```text
Give me this week's government activity that affects my area, across ALL levels —
local (city/county), state/province, and national/federal. Cover:
- major news involving my city / county / state / national government
- concrete government ACTIONS (votes, orders, rulings, contracts signed) at any level
- projects (started, changed, completed)
- budgets (allocations, overruns, savings) — local, state, and national lines that reach me
- investigations or audits
- appointments and departures
- public meetings held
- state or national laws / decisions that materially affect my locality or interests
Tag each item's level (local / state / national); when it's above local, add one
line on how it reaches me. Weight toward my stated interests and toward what
actually affects my area over distant national noise. Separate fact from
interpretation. Cite a source for each item. Keep it to the ~10 most significant items.
RECENCY GUARD: date-stamp every item. Include only items from the past 7-10
days. Web search often surfaces older stories that look current - if an item
is older or undated, either drop it or label it clearly ("[from <month>]" /
"[undated - verify]"). Never present old news as this week's news.
```

---

Part of [Citizen Copilot](../README.md). Adapt the prompt to your locality and
assistant; keep the "separate fact from opinion / prefer official sources" rules.
