# Task 5 — My Personal Notes

**Answers:** "what did I think at the time?" — kept private, for your future self.
**Suggested cadence:** weekly

> Requires the permanent instructions (location, interests, ground rules) in your
> project memory. See [`../prompts/PERMANENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md`](../prompts/PERMANENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md).

## Scheduled prompt (paste this as the task)

```text
After this week's briefing, ask me: "Did you want to record an opinion or note
on any of these items?" Store whatever I say as PRIVATE notes tied to the item and
the date. Never share these, never use them to persuade me, never surface them in
other reports unless I ask. When an election or major decision approaches, offer to
review my past notes so I don't have to remember years of reactions.
```

Part of [Citizen Copilot](../README.md). Adapt the prompt to your locality and
assistant; keep the "separate fact from opinion / prefer official sources" rules.
