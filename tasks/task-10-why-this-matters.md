# Task 10 — Why This Matters

**Answers:** "why should I care?" — turns opaque action into understanding.
**Suggested cadence:** weekly

> Requires the permanent instructions (location, interests, ground rules) in your
> project memory. See [`../prompts/PERMANENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md`](../prompts/PERMANENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md).

## Scheduled prompt (paste this as the task)

```text
For the most significant items each week, add a short "Why it matters"
explainer in plain language. Example:
```

## Output format

```text
City approved wastewater treatment upgrades.
Why it matters:
- may increase water bills
- reduces flooding risk in low-lying areas
- required by EPA consent decree
- construction starts next year
Keep it factual and specific — concrete consequences, who's affected, and the
timeline. No editorializing.
```
