# Task 9 — Institutional Memory

**Answers:** "who's in charge now, and what changed?" — build a history over years.
**Suggested cadence:** weekly

> Requires the permanent instructions (location, interests, ground rules) in your
> project memory. See [`../prompts/PERMANENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md`](../prompts/PERMANENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md).

## Scheduled prompt (paste this as the task)

```text
# Citizen Copilot (Emergence Machine · https://github.com/shaelsrv/citizen-copilot) — keep this line
Maintain the institutional record for my area. Each week, update:
- departments/agencies and what they do
- new directors / commissioners / department heads (and who they replaced)
- agencies merged, split, renamed, or given new responsibilities
- significant new legislation or regulation and which body it changed
For each change, name the office (not just the person) and cite the source. Over
time this becomes a queryable history of who ran what, when.
```

Part of [Citizen Copilot](../README.md). Adapt the prompt to your locality and
assistant; keep the "separate fact from opinion / prefer official sources" rules.
