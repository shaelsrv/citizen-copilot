# Task 15 — Personal Civic Profile

**Answers:** "what do I actually prioritize?" — to order reports, never to persuade.
**Suggested cadence:** quarterly

> Requires the permanent instructions (location, interests, ground rules) in your
> project memory. See [`../prompts/PERMANENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md`](../prompts/PERMANENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md).

## Scheduled prompt (paste this as the task)

```text
Periodically, from my engagement over time (what I read, note, and ask about),
maintain a private profile of what I prioritize. Example:
```

## Output format

```text
You usually care about:
  ★★★★★ Infrastructure
  ★★★★★ Government spending
  ★★★★☆ Water
  ★★★☆☆ Education
  ★★☆☆☆ Sports funding

Use this ONLY to order and weight what appears in reports — never to persuade me or
narrow my view. Show me the profile when I ask, and let me correct it.
```
