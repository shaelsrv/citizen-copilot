# Task 16 — Economy Watch

**Answers:** "how is the economy that I live in actually doing?"
**Suggested cadence:** monthly

> Requires the permanent instructions (location, interests, ground rules) in your
> project memory. See [`../prompts/PERMANENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md`](../prompts/PERMANENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md).

## Scheduled prompt (paste this as the task)

```text
Give me a plain, sourced read on the state of the economy that actually reaches me,
across ALL levels — local (city/county), state/province, and national/federal —
weighted toward what lands in my area. Cover, where official data exists:
- jobs & wages — local unemployment rate, hiring/layoffs by major employers,
  wage trends; new business openings/closures and permits
- cost of living — local inflation/CPI, rent and home prices, utility and transit
  fares, property-tax changes
- public finances that affect me — my city/county/state budget balance, deficits
  or surpluses, debt, credit rating, and any tax or fee changes
- money coming in — state/national grants, infrastructure funding, or schemes
  flowing to my locality; major public or private investment announced here
- the broader backdrop only as it reaches me — national growth, interest rates,
  or a national budget line, with one line on how it lands locally

For each point: give the number or fact, its level (local/state/national), the
exact period it covers, and the direction vs. the prior period (up/down/flat, and
whether that's new). Separate hard official data from estimates or forecasts, and
label which is which — a labeled "estimate" beats false precision. Prefer primary
sources (statistics bureau, labor department, budget office, central bank, city
finance page). Do NOT give investment, trading, or personal-finance advice, and do
NOT tell me the economy is "good" or "bad" — report the indicators and let me judge.
End with "Things worth watching": 2–3 indicators that could move soon.
```

---

Part of [Citizen Copilot](../README.md). Adapt the prompt to your locality and
assistant; keep the "separate fact from opinion / prefer official sources" rules.
