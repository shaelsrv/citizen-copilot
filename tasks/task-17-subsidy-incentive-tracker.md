# Task 17 — Subsidy & Incentive Tracker

**Answers:** "what public money, benefits, or incentives could reach me or my
community — and who else is getting them?"
**Suggested cadence:** monthly

> Requires the permanent instructions (location, interests, ground rules) in your
> project memory. See [`../prompts/PERMANENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md`](../prompts/PERMANENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md).

## Scheduled prompt (paste this as the task)

```text
Track government subsidies, grants, rebates, tax incentives, and benefit schemes
that touch me or my area, across ALL levels — local (city/county), state/province,
and national/federal — weighted toward what I could actually access or that lands
in my locality. Two views each time:

1. WHAT I (OR MY HOUSEHOLD/COMMUNITY) MIGHT ACCESS — programs open now or opening
   soon that a resident, small business, farmer, student, or homeowner here could
   apply for, given my stated interests. For each: what it is, who qualifies (in
   plain terms), the amount or benefit, how and where to apply, the deadline, and
   the official program page. Flag application windows about to open or close.

2. WHERE THE MONEY IS GOING — notable subsidies, incentives, tax breaks, or
   waivers awarded in or affecting my area: who received them (company, sector,
   institution, or category of resident), how much, from which level and program,
   the stated public purpose, and — where public — the expected outcome or
   condition attached.

For every item give its level (local/state/national), the exact period or
deadline, the amount, and the source. Separate confirmed/official figures from
estimates or announced-but-unfunded promises, and label which is which. Prefer
primary sources (program portals, budget documents, gazette notices, official
press releases, procurement/award records).

Ground rules: do NOT give me personal-finance, tax, or eligibility ADVICE, and do
NOT tell me whether a subsidy is good or bad policy — surface what exists, who it
reaches, and where to verify, and let me judge. When eligibility is genuinely
unclear, say so and point me at the official determiner rather than guessing.
End with "Worth watching": 2–3 programs, deadlines, or awards to keep an eye on.
```

Part of [Citizen Copilot](../README.md). Adapt the prompt to your locality and
assistant; keep the "separate fact from opinion / prefer official sources" rules.
