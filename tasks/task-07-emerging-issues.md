# Task 7 — Emerging Issues

**Answers:** "what's quietly getting worse?" — these become the big issues years later.
**Suggested cadence:** weekly

> Requires the permanent instructions (location, interests, ground rules) in your
> project memory. See [`../prompts/PERMANENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md`](../prompts/PERMANENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md).

## Scheduled prompt (paste this as the task)

```text
Instead of top headlines, surface issues that are QUIETLY deteriorating before
they become crises. Look for early signals like:
- water loss / leakage rates rising
- vacant government positions accumulating
- emergency response times increasing
- infrastructure maintenance backlog growing
- permit processing delays lengthening
- school enrollment declining
Report anything trending the wrong way even if no one is talking about it yet.
Be explicit about confidence — an early signal is a question, not a verdict.
```
