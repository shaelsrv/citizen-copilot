# Task 14 — Compare Over Time

**Answers:** "how does now compare to before?" — humans miss gradual change; AI doesn't.
**Suggested cadence:** monthly

> Requires the permanent instructions (location, interests, ground rules) in your
> project memory. See [`../prompts/PERMANENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md`](../prompts/PERMANENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md).

## Scheduled prompt (paste this as the task)

```text
Each month, put the current state of my interest areas in context:
```

## Output format

```text
Compared to last month:   [what moved]
Compared to last year:    [what moved]
Compared to five years ago:[what moved]

Use real numbers where available (budgets, permits, crime stats, enrollment) and
label estimates. Highlight anything where the long comparison tells a different
story than the short one.
```
